## My Writing Process

My general approach is to first understand the audience and purpose of the content.

I then identify the main problem, need or message.

After that, I develop the main idea and write the first version.

I review the content for clarity, grammar, structure, tone and readability.

For marketing copy, I also consider the hook, benefit, value proposition and call to action.

When appropriate, I create multiple versions so different messaging angles can be compared.

## AI-Assisted Workflow

I use AI tools such as ChatGPT to support research, brainstorming, outlining, drafting and editing.

However, I do not treat AI output as the finished product.

I review the material, make decisions about the messaging, improve the structure, check clarity and adjust the tone before considering the work complete.

The goal is to use AI as a productivity and research tool while maintaining human judgment over the final content.
