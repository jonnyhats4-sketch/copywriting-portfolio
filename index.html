How I work

Research first, edit last. Every line is written from confirmed facts and checked by hand, whether it started as my draft or came from a tool.

Research the facts.Product details, competitors, and how customers describe the problem. I write only from what is confirmed.
Find the customer insight.Who is buying, what is stopping them, and what would make the decision easy.
Choose the angle.One clear idea to lead with: the problem, the routine, or the payoff.
Draft in their words.Headlines and body copy in the customer's language. Tools can speed up a first pass.
Edit every line.Facts, grammar, tone, and anything that does not earn its place, checked by hand.
Refine until it's ready.A final read for clarity and consistency, so the copy is ready to publish.

A copywriter who starts with the customer.

I'm a copywriter and copy editor with 3 years of experience, focused on e-commerce, advertising and conversion copy. I start with what the customer needs and what is stopping them, then write it plainly and edit it hard. Every project is labelled so you can see exactly what it is. I'd rather show how I think than inflate a client list.

I write digital copy for the places customers actually decide: marketplace listings, product pages, social ads, landing pages and emails. People scan on their phones and give you a few seconds, so every headline, bullet and call to action gets one clear job.

I'm also a copy editor. I'll tighten your draft, fix the grammar and bring the tone in line with your brand, without changing what you mean.
