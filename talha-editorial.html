01
Client Project — Real Client

Maison Lumara

Jewelry / Amazon + Daraz Marketplace Copy

Brief
Marketplace listings for a minimal jewelry brand on Amazon and Daraz.
Objective
Keep one brand identity while adapting the writing to how shoppers browse each platform.
Audience
Shoppers who prefer simple, understated jewelry for themselves or as a gift.
Customer problem
Jewelry listings often shout for attention. The intended customer wants something quiet, simple and easy to wear.
Strategy
Keep the brand calm and refined. Amazon: structured, search-aware, comparison-friendly. Daraz: shorter, mobile-friendly, plain and friendly.
Confirmed facts
Brand: Maison Lumara. Brand line: Beauty · Elegance · You. Minimal jewelry: pendants, lockets and rings. Platforms: Amazon and Daraz.
Amazon · Structured

Title

"Maison Lumara Minimalist Jewelry | Pendant, Locket and Ring | Simple Everyday Style"

  • Minimal design: Clean, simple styling that keeps focus on you.
  • Pendants, lockets and rings: Choose a piece that suits you, from a quiet pendant to a locket that holds meaning.
  • Easy to wear: Understated enough to pair with everyday outfits.
  • A thoughtful gift: Simple and personal for birthdays, anniversaries or just because.
  • Maison Lumara: Minimal jewelry with a simple idea behind it: beauty, elegance and you.

"Maison Lumara makes minimal jewelry for people who prefer less. Choose from pendants, lockets and rings in a simple, understated style that fits into everyday life. Wear it on its own or add it to the pieces you already own."

Daraz · Short, mobile-first

Title

"Maison Lumara Minimal Pendant, Locket and Ring | Simple Everyday Jewellery"

Short description: "Simple, understated jewellery for everyday wear. Pendants, lockets and rings."

  • Minimal, understated design
  • Pendants, lockets and rings to choose from
  • Easy to wear every day
  • A simple gift idea

"Looking for jewellery that's simple and easy to wear? Maison Lumara brings you minimal pendants, lockets and rings that go with everyday outfits. Pick your style and wear it daily, or give it to someone who likes understated pieces."

Social concept · Single image

"Less, but yours."

Caption: "Minimal jewellery for people who like things simple. Pendants, lockets, rings."

CTA: Shop the collection.

Email concept

"Simple pieces for every day."

Preview: "Pendants, lockets and rings from Maison Lumara."

"Some days call for a statement. Most days don't. Maison Lumara is minimal jewellery for the days in between."

CTA: Shop the collection

Only confirmed information is used. No materials, purity, dimensions, warranty, shipping claims or results are stated.

Why it works

The same brand idea is adapted to different platforms instead of copying one listing everywhere. Amazon receives more structured marketplace copy, while Daraz receives shorter mobile-first copy.

Portfolio Project

FlowPilot

SaaS / B2B Conversion Copy

Brief
Landing-page messaging for a workflow-management product concept.
Audience
Team leads and managers coordinating tasks across people.
Customer problem
Work gets lost between chats, spreadsheets and meetings. Nobody is sure who owns what, deadlines become unclear, and managers spend time chasing status updates.
Objective
Name the problem, position FlowPilot as the answer, and earn one small action from the visitor.
Strategy
Use the reader's own language to describe the pain. Position FlowPilot as one place where tasks, owners and deadlines live. Give every section one clear job.
Product
FlowPilot is a product concept. It is not a client.

Stop chasing updates. Start finishing work.

FlowPilot keeps every task, owner and deadline in one place, so Monday's meeting is about what's done, not who's doing it.

Messaging 01

Know who owns what

Every task has a name and a date.

Messaging 02

Skip the status chase

See where work stands without asking.

Messaging 03

One place for the team

Fewer chats. Fewer spreadsheets.

CTA: Try FlowPilot

B2B buyer journey (concept)
01

Awareness

Name the problem in the reader's own words so they recognise it.

02

Consideration

Explain what FlowPilot is and what changes for the reader.

03

Evaluation

Build confidence over time and help them judge fit.

04

Action

Make the next step small and clear: try, book, start.

Formats

Solid outline: portfolio work. Dashed: conceptual / speculative formats, not client work.

Landing pageHeadline & subheadlineValue propositionsProduct messagingCTAEmail sequenceSales enablementCase-study-style messagingAd / message concepts

Why it works

The headline names the operational pain instead of describing generic software features. The supporting copy translates product functionality into a clear business outcome.

Portfolio Project

SONIQ

Consumer Advertising / Ad Copy

Brief
Ad hooks and primary text for a consumer audio product concept.
Audience
Everyday listeners who care about how music, calls and videos sound.
Customer problem
Flat, forgettable sound makes everyday listening feel like background noise.
Objective
Stop the scroll with a strong first line and move the reader toward a click.
Strategy
Three different creative angles around the same product. Each hook works independently while pointing to the same benefit.
Note
SONIQ is a product concept. No campaign results are claimed.
Angle 01 · FrustrationTired of music that sounds like it's in another room?

Primary: "SONIQ is for people who actually listen. Put it on and hear what you've been missing."

Purpose: Name a problem the listener already feels.

Why it works: It starts from the reader's frustration, not the product.

Hear it for yourself
Angle 02 · RoutineYour commute has a soundtrack. Make it a good one.

Primary: "Turn travel time into your time with SONIQ."

Purpose: Attach the product to a moment already in the day.

Why it works: The reader pictures using it straight away.

Shop SONIQ
Angle 03 · CuriosityPress play. Notice the difference.

Primary: "One listen is all it takes to hear what SONIQ is about."

Purpose: Invite a trial without over-explaining.

Why it works: A short, direct line suits a fast scroll.

Try SONIQ
Why it works

Short hooks create different entry points for different audiences. The same product can be tested through frustration, routine and curiosity rather than relying on one message.

Everyday Carry

E-commerce / Product Copy

Brief
A complete product page for a backpack concept.
Audience
People who carry their essentials every day.
Customer problem
Daily bags get overstuffed and disorganized, and finding anything means digging through the bottom of the bag.
Objective
Turn product features into reasons to buy and make the product page easy to scan.
Strategy
Lead with the everyday problem, then show how each feature removes friction.
Note
Product concept. No specifications are stated.

One bag for everything you carry every day.

Grab it, go, and know where everything is.

Feature → benefit

Made for daily carry

Built around the things you take every day, not the trip you take once a year.

Feature → benefit

Easier to organise

Give each item a place so you stop digging at the bottom of the bag.

Feature → benefit

Simple to live with

A clean, no-fuss look that goes with what you're wearing.

"Most days, your bag does the same job: it carries your essentials from home to wherever you're going. The Everyday Carry Backpack is built around that routine, so packing is quick and finding things is easier."

CTA: Shop the Everyday Carry Backpack

Why it works

Each feature is translated into an outcome the buyer can picture. The copy stays within the product information available rather than inventing specifications.
